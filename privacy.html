Privacy Policy

Last updated November 11, 2026

What we collect

When you request a free preview, we collect the information you give us directly: your name, phone number (optional), and email address. If you go on to use the service, we also collect the photos you choose to send us—both the photos of your own face used as the reference for your swaps, and the original photos you want transformed.

How we use it

We use your contact information to send you your private upload link, your preview, and updates about your order. We use your photos only to produce your face-swapped previews and final images. Every swap is reviewed by a human on our team before it's sent to you—we don't ship anything automated and unreviewed.

Photo retention and deletion

Your photos are used only for the order you submitted them for. We do not use your photos to train any shared or general-purpose model, and we do not reuse them for any other customer's order.

  • Finished files are retained only as long as needed to deliver your order and handle any revisions you request.
  • All source photos are deleted within 30 days of delivery.
  • You can request earlier deletion at any time by emailing us—see Contact below.

Consent and likeness

We only produce face swaps using your own likeness, or a face you confirm you are authorized to use with documented consent from that person. We may ask you to confirm this before starting work. This policy exists to keep the service from being used to impersonate or deceive someone without their knowledge.

Cookies and analytics

This site uses the Meta (Facebook) Pixel to measure the performance of our advertising and understand how visitors reach our preview request form. It records page visits and form submissions, and may set cookies in your browser. We use this information in aggregate to run and improve our ads—we don't use it to identify you individually outside of that. You can control cookies through your browser settings, and opt out of interest-based Meta ads at facebook.com/adpreferences.

We also use PostHog, a product analytics tool, to understand how visitors use this site—for example, which steps of the preview request form people complete or abandon. This may include session recordings of on-site activity (mouse movement, clicks, and page navigation) and can set cookies in your browser. We use this to improve the site, not to build an advertising profile of you.

Who we share it with

We don't sell your information or your photos. We use a small number of service providers to operate the business—for example, secure database hosting to store your contact details, hosting infrastructure to run this website, and Meta for advertising analytics as described above. These providers only process data on our behalf and don't have independent rights to use it.

Payment

You are never charged until you review and approve your private preview. We do not store full payment card details ourselves; payment is handled by a third-party payment processor at the time you approve your order.

Your rights

You can ask us what information we hold about you, correct it, or ask us to delete it—including your photos—at any time. To do so, email us at the address below.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the service after a change means you accept the updated policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Email contact@faceswap.fit.

Note: this policy is written in plain language to explain how we actually handle your information. It isn't a substitute for independent legal advice.
